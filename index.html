About Idea Incubator

Idea Incubator MGIT, is a student club focused on fostering Leadership in innovation and research amongst MGITans. It was founded on 17th April 2019 by the Principal, MGIT as its de facto head. The club conducts Ideathons, Design thinking contests to help students nurture ideas to develop cutting edge technology.

The core developer team build deployable apps and works on ideas and also has a banger team for external technical events like hackathons etc.

Mission: The mission of the club is to work on NextGen Projects, collaborate with professionals and companies to enrich research skills, develop an entrepreneurial mindset with professional skills to stand out of the crowd as gems of MGIT and revered researchers of the nation at large.

Our Ecosystem

Three pillars that power innovation at MGIT

Government Initiative

IIC — Institution's Innovation Council

Established by MGIT to promote innovation and entrepreneurship among faculty and students. The IIC fosters a culture of innovation on campus through workshops, events, mentorship, and industry linkages — nurturing ideas into startups and strengthening MGIT's participation in national innovation activities.

Visit IIC Page ↗
🚀
Entrepreneurship

EDC — Entrepreneurship Development Cell

The EDC at MGIT is built on the belief that entrepreneurs can be developed through well-directed activities. It motivates students through EDPs and skill development, fosters industry–R&D linkages, and promotes techno-entrepreneurship for wealth creation and employment across the region.
